ZabZaab is a somtum-making cooking simulator game that aims to represent Thai culture in the form of one of your childhood cooking games. Tum somtum with different ingredients to earn money, and experience the feeling of owning a local street food stall in suburban Thailand. _Because we all have Plara in our veins._ This game is a part of our final project in the Game Studies and AI course in the International Summer Academy of the University of Toronto, instructor Aileen Dong. This project is open-source and available under the [MIT License](LICENSE). #zapzaap
